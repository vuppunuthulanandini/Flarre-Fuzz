Personal auto premium rules:
1. Base premium is 500.
2. Driver age under 25: multiply by 1.5.
3. Driver age 70 or over: multiply by 1.3.
4. Drivers aged 25 to 29 inclusive with 2 or more claims: multiply by 1.10 (high-risk surcharge).
5. Each claim adds 10% to the premium, capped at +50% in total.
6. Safe-driver discount is 10%. Multi-policy discount is 15%. Total discount is capped at 20%.
7. Final premium is rounded to 2 decimal places.
